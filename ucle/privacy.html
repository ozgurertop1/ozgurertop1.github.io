Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Version 1.1 · Türkçe

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Üçle! mobile game ("the App").

In short: There is no account; we never ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your phone. From version 1.1, anonymous gameplay statistics (such as which level was played, won or lost) are sent to our server with a random install number that does not identify you, so we can improve the game. There are no ads.

1. Who we are

Özgür Ertop (individual developer), Türkiye · Email: birharfbirsoru.app@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your phone

DataPurposeWhere
Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, coins, lives, boosters, shop progress, Daily Puzzle streakLetting you continue where you left offOnly on your phone
Profile: the name and picture you chooseShowing them on the profile screenOnly on your phone; never shared
Settings: sound, vibration, reminders, languageRemembering your preferencesOnly on your phone

This data is removed when you delete the App. You can also erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress.

3. Anonymous gameplay statistics

To see which levels are too hard and whether players enjoy the game (whether they come back the next day), the App sends the following to our server. There is no account, and this data is not linked to your name, email, phone number or advertising ID.

DataDetails
Install numberA random number the App creates on first launch. It is not your device ID and is not linked to you; if you delete and reinstall the App, a new number is created.
Gameplay eventsApp opened, screen viewed, level started / won / lost / left, booster used, level time and stars.
Device infoOperating system (iOS / Android), app version, game language and the country code from your phone's region setting.

Your IP address is not written to these records. Records are deleted automatically after 180 days. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Records are stored with our hosting provider Supabase on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt).

4. Reminder notifications

Daily Puzzle, streak and "lives full" reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. No notification token is sent to any server. You can turn reminders off in Settings → Reminders or in your phone's notification settings.

5. Store ratings

If you choose to rate the game, the rating window belongs to the App Store or Google Play, and your rating is subject to that store's privacy policy.

6. Sharing

Anonymous gameplay statistics are used only to improve the game; they are never shared or sold (see section 3 for our hosting provider). The store you downloaded the App from (Apple App Store, Google Play) processes data for its own services under its own policy.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the CCPA in California or the KVKK in Türkiye) you have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because the statistics are anonymous and not linked to you, we cannot match them to a person, but you can write to birharfbirsoru.app@gmail.com with any question; we answer within 30 days.

9. Changes

If a new feature (for example ads, online backup or crash reports) is added, this policy will be updated before that feature ships and the date at the top will change.
