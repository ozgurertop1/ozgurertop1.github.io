Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the mobile game Kader Sahası: Futbol Kariyeri (Destiny Field, "the App").

In short: The App collects no personal data and does not connect to the internet. There are no accounts; no email, phone number, location, contacts or photos are requested. There are no ads, analytics or in-app purchases. Your career is stored only on your phone.

1. Data controller

Özgür Ertop (individual) · Email: birharfbirsoru.app@gmail.com

2. Information stored on your phone

InformationPurposeWhere
Player profile: the name, nickname, appearance and finger-drawn signature you chooseShowing your character in the gameOnly on your phone; never shared
Career progress: seasons, matches, decisions, statisticsLetting you continue where you left offOnly on your phone
Tip preferences: which tips you have seenNot repeating the same tipOnly on your phone

This information is deleted from your phone when you uninstall the App. Choosing Start a New Story at the end of a career also deletes the save.

3. App stores

The store you download the App from (Apple App Store, Google Play) processes data for its own service under its own policy.

4. Sharing

Because the App collects no personal data, nothing is shared, sold or transferred abroad.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your rights and contact

Send any privacy question to birharfbirsoru.app@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

7. Changes

If a feature that processes data (such as ads, online backup or crash reporting) is added, this policy will be updated before that release and the date above will change.
